# IILM University - Knowledge Base

## About IILM University
IILM University, Greater Noida is a private university established under the Uttar Pradesh Private Universities Act.
Founded in 2001 under the Ram Krishan & Sons Charitable Trust.
Over 30 years of academic legacy in developing leaders.
Campus spans 26 acres in Knowledge Park II, Greater Noida.
Website: https://iilm.edu/greater-noida/
